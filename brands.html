Four directions

Chosen: 02, Ember & Graphite — with a hexagon/circle shape blend and occasional saturated flavour. This page is kept as the record of what was on the table.

What stays whatever you pick: four tabs at the bottom, one fixed accent that never changes between tabs, a single reserved reward colour, and plain phrasing. The brand changes the surface, not the rules.

My call, if you want one: 02, Ember & Graphite. It suits what the app is actually for — starting things, riding, building a business — better than the soft green does, and the hard edges make it read as a tool rather than a wellness app. Its one problem is that ember and gold are neighbours, so I would cool the reward to a pale brass to keep the gold meaning what it means.

Reply with a number and I will apply it everywhere in one pass.
